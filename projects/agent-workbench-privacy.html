Privacy

Agent Workbench Native has no AWBN account, advertising, cloud sync, or built-in telemetry.

Stored locally

Chats, attachments, settings, registered project paths, optional saved keys, and artifacts are stored under ~/.config/agent-workbench-native/. Registered projects may also contain a gitignored .agent-workbench/ folder. AWBN does not encrypt these files; use full-disk encryption where needed.

What can leave your machine

Prompts and attached context sent through cloud-backed CLIs go to that provider under your account and terms. Custom clients, model tools, MCP servers, connectors, and update checks can make their own network requests. Ollama inference is local, but enabled tools may not be.

Deletion

Deleting a chat removes AWBN-owned session data and artifacts. Provider-native history is controlled separately. To erase everything, close AWBN and its bridge, remove ~/.config/agent-workbench-native/, and remove .agent-workbench/ folders from registered projects.

Share carefully

Do not paste credentials into chat or custom commands. Raw Inspect output, logs, diffs, and screenshots can contain source code, prompts, paths, remotes, or secrets. Review and redact before sharing.
