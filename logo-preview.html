Three proper treatments

The last five were your terminal font with the colour changed, which is why they looked basic. These use real display typefaces and treat the word as an object rather than as coloured text.

One idea underneath all three: the name sounds forged, your site is already a dark workshop, and orange on black is what hot metal looks like. So the heat runs through the letters rather than glowing around them.
